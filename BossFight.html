Boss Fight

Fall Semester 2026

Boss Fight was a Machine Learning course project developed by a team of five over six weeks. Our proposal was to create a boss in a Soulslike game that learns how to fight the player. To achieve this goal, we built a game in Unity and trained various AI boss bots. We ended with a model that is both difficult and interesting to fight against.

Training Montage

Process

Game: For this project, the team created a simple fighting game in the Unity Engine. The game has just the player, boss, and arena. Player can move, light attack, heavy attack, and roll. Boss can choose betweeen move, quick attack, AOE attack, and shoot projectiles.

Boss: We compiled game state as a vector and trained boss bots by letting them fight simulated player bots. The boss bots were trained under the reward system; like they get points for winning and lose points for losing. Their goal was to figure out how to get maximum reward consistently.

Player: The player bots were initially hard-coded, but we eventually also trained the player bots to improve boss bots. We trained them through imitation learning, which was feeding our footages of fighting the boss for them to learn.

Tool: The boss models were created with the ML-Agents Package of Unity, which uses Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) neural network. Its advantage is that it ensures relatively smooth and stable learning.
