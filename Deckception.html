Deckception

Fall Semester 2025

Real Life Party Card Game


Description

It is graduation season at the Master Spy Academy. To graduate, all spies must complete their assigned secret missions—without revealing their involvement. But nothing stays hidden for long. Every card you take is public, every move is watched, and every decision could expose your true mission. Can you successfully deduce everyone else’s mission while keeping your own secret?

-- Deckception was a real life party card game I developed in a team of four.

-- We were restricted to create a game offline and can only utilize a few simple materials like a deck of cards and paper.

-- The goal was to fully understand the art of Play and design a truly fun game.

Check out the website at: https://wallycodesgames.itch.io/deckception
