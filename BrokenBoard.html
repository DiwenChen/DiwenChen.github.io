Broken Board

Fall Semester 2025

3D Skateboarding Simulator


Trailer



Description

Broken Board is a skateboarding game where you’re being pursued by a monster through a decrepit abandoned factory, and all you can do is skate!
The more stylish you are, the faster you go, and the faster you go, the longer you can survive. Be cool or die.

-- Broken Board was my first Unreal and big 3D game project, developed within 7 weeks.

-- I worked on many aspects of the game while specialized in UI Tech and special effects.

-- The game definitely needs more polishing, But I am proud of it as my first big 3D game.
