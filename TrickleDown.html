Gameplay Video



Game Description

Trickle Down Reaganomics is a 2D One-Button game where the player tries to get the falling trash into the wall pipe chutes.
-- You are a Trash Collector of the Trash Collect Inc.
-- In this world, rich people throw really wasteful parties and toss rash out the window.
-- Your job is to collect those trash into the wall pipe chutes using the anti-gravity field.
-- But whatever you do, JUST DO NOT SEND THE TRASH BACK UP!

Process

-- The game is a course project developed by a team of three people over five weeks.
-- The project constraint is that the game can only be played with one button.
-- I was responsible for the Narrative, UI, System Design, and Special Effects.
